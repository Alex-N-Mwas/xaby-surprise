Put your music files in this folder. The page will play them in filename order after Xaby opens the surprise.

Supported formats: MP3, M4A, MP4 audio, OGG, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OPUS, and WEBM.

For example:

- `01 - your song.mp3`
- `02 - another song.mp3`

Filenames are only used to sort tracks. They are not shown on the page. If this folder has no supported audio files, the page plays its soft built-in instrumental instead.
